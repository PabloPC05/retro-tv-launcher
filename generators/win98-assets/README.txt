  Original fonts: FontStructions "MS Sans Serif" and "MS Sans Serif Bold" by "lou" on FontStruct,
          https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384746 and .../1384862
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0),
          http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
          -> keep attribution to "lou" (FontStruct); modified/derived fonts must stay CC BY-SA.
  Note: pixel font, designed for ~11px (use 11/22/33 px etc. with anti-aliasing off for crispness;
        bold is a separate family).  98.css itself is MIT (Copyright 2020 Jordan Scales).
